FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Global Radio really free?

Yes. The app is free with no ads. All core content — daily astrology, stories, devotion, news — is included.

Do I need an account?

No. The app works fully without signing in. Optional Google/Apple sign-in only adds cross-device sync of your preferences.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Save any item and it plays without internet — handy for commutes and low-network areas.

Where does the content come from?

A mix of original scripts written for Global Radio and retellings of public-domain classics like the Panchatantra, narrated with neural text-to-speech. Full source credits are published in our attribution registry.

Is it safe for children?

Kids Mode restricts the app to child-friendly stories only. We collect no personal information from children.
