Common questions

Audio won't play or keeps buffering

Check your internet connection first. If you're offline, only items you've saved will play. If the problem persists on Wi-Fi, force-close and reopen the app — and make sure you're on the latest version.

How do I change my language or interests?

Open the You tab → Settings → Languages / Interests. Changes apply to your radio immediately.

How do I save content for offline listening?

Tap the save icon on any item. Saved items appear in your Library and play without internet.

How do I turn Kids Mode on or off?

Open the You tab and use the Kids Mode switch. The app turns pink and shows only child-friendly stories.

How do I stop daily notifications?

You tab → Settings → Notifications, or disable notifications for Global Radio in your device settings.

Delete your account & data

You can delete your account inside the app (You → Settings → Account → Delete account), or email privacy@globalradio.app from your registered address with the subject "Delete my data". Account data is deleted within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Report a content issue

If you believe any content is inaccurate, inappropriate, or infringes rights, email legal@globalradio.app with the item title and language. We review all reports.
